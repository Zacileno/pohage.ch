What we repair

From a small fault to an emergency

Boiler faults

Repairs of wood, coal and pellet boilers including control electronics.

Heat pump repairs

Diagnostics and repair of the refrigeration circuit and the compressor.

Air conditioning repairs

Sealing of leaks, refilling of refrigerant and repair of the units.

Emergency call-out

Call-out when heating or cooling fails.

Spare parts

We source original spare parts for the brands we represent.

Repair warranty

How the repair works

From the fault report to commissioning

1

Reporting the fault

You describe the problem by phone, by e-mail or via the form.

2

Diagnostics

We identify the cause of the fault and prepare a quotation.

3

Repair

Once you approve the quotation we carry out the repair and source spare parts if needed.

4

Commissioning

We test the system and hand it over in working order, including recommendations for the future.

Frequently asked questions

Repairs – questions and answers

How quickly do you come out for a heating emergency?

For emergencies we arrange the earliest possible appointment, as a rule within 24 hours.

Do I get a quotation in advance?

Yes, after the diagnostics we prepare a quotation which you approve before the repair begins.

Do you also repair units from other manufacturers?

Yes, we also carry out diagnostics and basic repairs on systems outside the brands we represent.

Do you provide a warranty on the repair?

Yes, we provide a warranty on the repairs carried out and on the spare parts used.
