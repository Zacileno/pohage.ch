Sales – questions and answers

Do you advise on the choice between a boiler and a heat pump?

Yes, we recommend a solution that matches the condition of the property, the energy consumption and your budget.

Do you also handle the installation, or only the delivery?

We handle the complete delivery and installation with our own technicians, including commissioning.

Do you help with financing or subsidies?

Yes, we advise on instalment options and available subsidy programmes.

How long do delivery and installation take?

The date depends on the type of system and current availability. We state the exact date in the quotation.
