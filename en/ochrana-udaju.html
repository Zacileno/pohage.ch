Privacy policy

How we process your personal data when you use our website or contact us through a form.

1. General

This privacy policy explains how PoHaGe GmbH processes personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP; SR 235.1) and the Data Protection Ordinance (DPO; SR 235.11).

Our website is designed to collect as little data as possible. You can visit it without providing any personal data. We only process the details you enter when you contact us through a form or book a callback.

2. Controller

For questions about data protection, contact us at this address or by email.

3. Consent and withdrawal

When you send us a message through the contact form or choose time slots in the callback form, you consent to us processing your details as described in this policy.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, write to info@pohage.ch stating your name and email address.

4. Website and services used

Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you visit it, technical data is stored automatically: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, and the date and time of the request. This data is used for technical operation, security and error analysis. We do not combine it with other data and keep it only as long as necessary for these purposes.

SSL/TLS encryption

The website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise it by "https://" in the address bar and the padlock icon in your browser.

Fonts

To display the typeface, we load Google Fonts from servers of Google LLC (USA). Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process.

Contact and callback forms

When you submit a form, we process your name, phone number, email address, your request or the time slots you chose, and the language of the page. The details are stored in a Google spreadsheet and forwarded to our team by email (Google Workspace by Google LLC, USA). We use the data only to handle your request and to contact you.

Access is limited to our staff and to the service providers we have engaged to maintain the website and the forms. We only pass data on to other third parties if this is necessary to handle your request or required by law.

Cookies and web analytics

We do not use cookies or any analytics or advertising services.

5. Disclosure abroad

The service providers named above may also process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the USA and the EU. Where data is disclosed to a country without an adequate level of data protection, we rely on appropriate safeguards under Art. 16 et seq. FADP, in particular standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by statutory retention obligations.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you have in particular the following rights:

  • Access to the personal data processed (Art. 25 FADP)
  • Release or transfer of the personal data (Art. 28 FADP)
  • Correction of inaccurate personal data (Art. 32 FADP)
  • Deletion or destruction of the personal data, unless statutory retention obligations prevent it (Art. 32 FADP)
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future

Please send requests to info@pohage.ch.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You can contact us at any time about data protection matters. The competent supervisory authority in Switzerland is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch.
