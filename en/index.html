Our suppliers

The manufacturers we represent

These manufacturers produce in Czechia and in Switzerland. We supply, install, service and repair their systems.

Ventilation systems and heat recovery

Czech manufacturer of ventilation units with heat recovery, for single-family houses, flats, schools and commercial premises. The units cover an air flow of 200 to 15'000 m³/h and return the heat from the extract air to the supply air. The company has been in business for over 30 years and delivers to 30 countries.
